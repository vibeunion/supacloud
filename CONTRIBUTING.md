# Contributions

New contributions to SupaCloud project-owned code are submitted under
Apache-2.0, as described in LICENSE and NOTICE. Submit only work you have
the right to contribute under those terms, and identify third-party code
and preserve its original license and attribution.

Contributors retain their copyright. This policy does not transfer copyright,
authorize future proprietary relicensing, or retroactively establish consent
for earlier contributions.

Before releasing the license transition, maintainers must verify the rights
and original license obligations of existing contributions. Previously
released versions retain the permissions granted with those versions.
